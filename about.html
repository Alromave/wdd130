About Calhuitz Rafting

Welcome to Calhuitz Rafting! We are dedicated to providing thrilling and safe rafting experiences for adventurers of all skill levels. Our team of experienced guides is passionate about sharing the beauty of nature and the excitement of white-water rafting.

Our mission is to create unforgettable memories while promoting environmental stewardship and safety on the water. Join us for an adventure you'll never forget!

History

For ten years, Calhuitz has been guiding guests through some of the most exciting whitewater while building a reputation defined by consistency, innovation, and an uncompromising commitment to safety. What began as a rafting-focused operation has grown into a broader adventure service provider, but the guiding principle remains the same: deliver thrilling experiences without ever compromising client wellbeing.
